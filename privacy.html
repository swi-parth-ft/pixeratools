Privacy

Privacy details for the Pixera website and app.

Pixera is built around an on-device screenshot workflow on macOS. This page covers what the website measures, what the app processes locally, and where third-party providers are involved.

Updated April 4, 2026 Questions: hello@pixeratools.com

Short Version

The app’s screenshot styling and redaction flow runs on your Mac.

  • Pixera’s screenshot processing workflow is positioned as on-device.
  • The marketing site uses Google Analytics for site and funnel measurement.
  • Checkout is handled off-domain through Lemon Squeezy.
  • Support questions can be sent to hello@pixeratools.com.

Website analytics

The Pixera website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and basic funnel behavior. Current website events include interactions such as guide opens, pricing views, checkout starts, installer downloads, and lead-generation clicks.

These analytics help measure which pages are being discovered and which calls to action are being used. They do not change how the app processes screenshots on your Mac.

App processing

Pixera is described as an offline macOS screenshot editor for styling, annotation, and privacy redaction. The product messaging and website documentation state that screenshot processing and redaction workflows are handled on-device.

If you use Pixera to redact emails, tokens, customer details, or other sensitive information, the intent of the workflow is that the screenshot is cleaned up before it is shared externally.

Checkout and payments

Pixera checkout is handled through Lemon Squeezy, which means payment and order processing happen off-domain. When you click into checkout, you are leaving the main marketing site and entering Lemon Squeezy’s checkout flow.

Please review Lemon Squeezy’s own privacy and payment terms if you need full details about their handling of checkout information.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Pixera, email hello@pixeratools.com.
