Why Docs Teams Care

Raw screenshots slow readers down when every step matters.

Documentation screenshots are not just decoration. They help a reader decide where to click, whether they are in the right place, and what changed between one step and the next. Pixera makes that layer easier to polish without opening a full design tool for every article update.

Onboarding guides

FAQ

Documentation screenshot questions

Do documentation screenshots need annotation on every image?

No. Add annotation only when the step is ambiguous. The goal is clarity, not marking up every screenshot the same way.

Should I redact screenshots that stay internal?

Often yes. Internal screenshots get reused later in docs, decks, tickets, and support articles more often than teams expect.

Can Pixera help release notes and changelog posts too?

Yes. The same styling and annotation workflow works well for release screenshots, changelogs, and public product updates.
